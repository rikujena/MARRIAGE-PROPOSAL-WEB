Chapter 1 of 5 · The Beginning

My Beautiful Girl

From the very first day we met, there is one thing I still cannot forget — your smile.

I did not know that smile would one day become one of my favorite things in this entire world.

You made my life beautiful in ways you probably do not even realize.

Chapter 2 of 5 · Precious Memories

The Little Things

I remember the little things — your laugh, the way you smile, our random conversations, those late-night talks when we had no idea how time was passing, those unexpected meetings that somehow became some of my favorite memories. Even the smallest moments with you became something I wanted to keep forever.

And yes… we have had our fights. We have misunderstood each other, said things we did not mean, gotten angry, and had moments when loving each other felt a little difficult.

And yes… we've had our little fights, misunderstandings, and dramatic moments too. Sometimes we drive each other crazy, but honestly, my heart has a funny habit. It saves all the beautiful memories and quietly deletes the bad ones.

Chapter 3 of 5 · My Unwavering Heart

I Choose You

Because whenever I look back at our journey, I don't see the fights first. I see you — the girl who made me smile when I did not feel like smiling. The girl who became my comfort without even trying. The girl whose presence can make an ordinary day feel special. The girl I can talk to about absolutely nothing and still never get bored.

And through all of it, there has always been one thing that never changed.

I choose you.

I choose you on the good days. I choose you on the difficult days. I choose you when we are laughing until we cannot breathe. I choose you when we are angry and do not want to talk. I choose you when everything feels perfect, and I choose you even when everything feels messy.

Chapter 4 of 5 · Forever Gratitude

What You Mean To Me

Because loving you was never just about the easy moments. It is about knowing that no matter how many times life changes, my heart will still find its way back to you.

Thank you for every moment you have given me. Thank you for every smile you brought into my life. Thank you for every conversation, every memory, every hug, every little effort, and every time you simply stayed.

You may never fully understand how much you mean to me. You are not just someone I love.

You are someone I want to build memories with. Someone I want to grow with. Someone I want to annoy for the rest of my life. Someone I want beside me when life is beautiful, and when life is difficult.

Chapter 5 of 5 · A Vow For Eternity

Until My Very Last Breath

And if life gave me the chance to go back and live everything again from the beginning, I would still choose the same path.

I would still look for that smile.

I would still want to meet you.

I would still fall for you.

And I would still choose you… today, tomorrow, every day after that, and until my very last breath.

I love you, my beautiful girl. ❤️ And no matter how many words I write, they will never be enough to explain what you mean to me.
